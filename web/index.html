Recall is 2-Δ/h. A concept's effective half-life is its own half-life multiplied by the recall of each prerequisite raised to γ, so γ = 0 makes every concept independent, which is what every spaced repetition system assumes. Half-lives here are illustrative, not fitted: the fitted numbers are in the study, and the README explains what they do and do not support.
